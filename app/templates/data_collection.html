Dual-Source Ingestion Pipeline

AtmosphereX initiates asynchronous non-blocking HTTP requests across global satellite arrays. In the event of network timeouts, rate-limiting (HTTP 429), or packet corruption, the pipeline automatically redirects streams through the Global Meteorological fallback mesh without packet loss.

Data Sanitization & Integrity Checks

All ingested records undergo immediate RFC-4180 validation, null-imputation, and meteorological physical boundary checks (e.g., verifying relative humidity remains strictly within 0% to 100%, and atmospheric pressure adheres to standard barometric ranges).
